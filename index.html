Decision Complexity and Trust in Large Language Model Advisors

with João Sedoc and Arun Sundararajan

Do people rely more on an LLM’s advice when a decision gets harder? In an incentive-compatible experiment, they do, but only when they can follow the advisor’s reasoning.

LLMs and Consumer Bias: “Paying Not to Go to the Gym” with an LLM Advisor?

with Sagit Bar-Gill and João Sedoc

In a field experiment with NYU Athletics, an LLM advisor given members’ past usage reinforced overconfident buyers, who went on to spend $17.7 more per month.

Catchy or Sketchy? How Thumbnail-Title Congruence Shapes Curiosity and Video Viewership

with Jaeung Sim, Daegon Cho, and Jiyong Eom

On video platforms, a thumbnail image that matches the title goes with more views, while matching thumbnail text goes with fewer. A gap sparks curiosity only when people expect it to be closed.
