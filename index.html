I study

The behavioral economics of human–AI interaction

AI is moving from systems that predict to advisors that talk with us. When a person and an AI go back and forth, the final decision comes out of that interaction, not the model alone. So, model quality is not necessarily decision quality. Through the lens of behavioral economics, I study AI advisors in their full decision context: what people bring to them, how the AI responds, whether people act on its advice, and whether they end up better off.
