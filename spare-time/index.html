SPARE TIME

I study how people decide with AI. I also make music with it.

Under the name Floating Bunny, I make songs with Suno, an AI music tool. Making music this way turns out to be a small, hands-on version of what I study: deciding how much to rely on what the AI gives back, when to push it in a different direction, and when to trust my own ear. Mostly, though, it is just a lot of fun.
