TEACHING

Helping students learn, with AI in the room

The core of my teaching is keeping students motivated to learn, which AI makes harder. I don’t ban AI: students use it anyway and will use it at work. I let them use it to support their learning and extend their projects, but I give them structure and test them without it so the learning sticks. I also try to teach with compassion and flexibility, because students start from different places and move at different speeds.
